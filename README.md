### About me

- 🗄️ I like the hard parts of databases: multi-terabyte PostgreSQL, billion-row tables, autovacuum tuning, online index builds, and connection pooling.
- 📨 I build event pipelines: high-volume consumers, batch inserts, prefetch/ack tuning, and autoscaling that follows the queue.
- 🤖 I build with LLMs end to end: RAG, agents and multi-agent architectures, tool use, fine-tuning, evaluation, and guardrails.
- 🧱 Polyglot by necessity and by taste: Go, TypeScript, Rust, .NET, Python, Kotlin, Swift, and more.
- 💙 Cross-platform apps are my soft spot: Flutter, React Native, Tauri, and Electron.
- 🧑‍🏫 I enjoy teaching and writing clear, step-by-step explanations.
- ☕ Coffee is necessary.
